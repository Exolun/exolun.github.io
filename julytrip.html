Decision Notes

How the options shake out for a July family weekend.

Pick North Bend for the easiest weekend

Moon River is the least disruptive choice: shortest drive, walkable North Bend setting, river access and three separate townhomes so families can spread out without separating completely.

  • Best fit if minimizing car time is the priority.
  • Strong family-zone setup with three kitchens and three hot tubs.
  • Most expensive option in the July search results.

Pick Sierra Lodge for the best balance

Sierra Lodge has the strongest blend of drive time, price, group capacity and built-in activities. The entertainment pavilion makes it the best rainy-day or mixed-age-kids option.

  • Good capacity buffer for 16 adults plus kids.
  • Hot tub, sauna, theater and indoor mini pickleball.
  • Confirm dog count and the no-events rule with the host.

Pick Spokane only if the constraints work

Deer's Run is the clear price and headcount winner on paper, but the long drive, stricter pet rule and apparent 3-night summer weekend minimum could outweigh the savings.

  • Lowest estimated share per family.
  • Largest stated sleeping capacity.
  • Likely needs a Thursday-Sunday booking instead of Friday-Sunday.

Before Booking

Quick confirmations that could change the decision.

  • Confirm the final checkout price for July 10-12, 2026, including taxes, cleaning, pet fees and service fees.
  • Confirm the minimum stay rules, especially for the Spokane listing.
  • Confirm that the full dog count is allowed. North Bend and Sierra Lodge list 2-pet limits; Spokane lists 1 dog under 20 lb.
  • Confirm the final headcount, parking limits and any vehicle registration requirements.
  • Confirm whether the gathering is treated as a normal family stay or an event under the host's rules.
