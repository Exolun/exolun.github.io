Decision Notes

How the options shake out for a multi-family weekend.

Pick Rockaway Beach for headcount and cost

The duplex is the practical choice if the group may approach 18-20 people. Two kitchens and two living areas also make it easier for families to split into smaller zones while still sharing one property.

  • Best per-person estimate.
  • Best bedroom count.
  • Easy beach access for kids and casual downtime.

Pick Gilded Elk for comfort and adult bedrooms

This is the polished option: 4 king bedrooms for adult couples, a large bunk room for kids, and the strongest reviews. It is also the most expensive and has the Suncadia pool limitation.

  • Best premium kitchen and living setup.
  • Great fit for roughly 4 families.
  • Confirm guest list and vehicle registration requirements before booking.

Pick Ronald for mountain amenities on a smaller scale

The Ronald cabin is a strong middle option for a smaller group that wants hot tub, games, outdoor space, and possible shared pool access without paying the Gilded Elk price.

  • Good family entertainment mix.
  • Best if 14 people is a real upper bound.
  • Ask about activity center fees and summer pool access timing.

Before Booking

Quick confirmations that could change the decision.

  • Confirm the final checkout price for June 26-28, 2026, including taxes, cleaning, pet fees, and service fees.
  • Confirm exact pet count, breed/size restrictions, and whether cats are allowed if relevant.
  • Confirm parking limits and whether all families can register vehicles easily.
  • Confirm quiet hours and any restrictions on large family gatherings, since all three listings say no events.
